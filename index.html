Meet Shalom

A braider first, a perfectionist always.

Shalom Nabwire is a professional stylist, public relations officer, digital marketing consultant and content creator. With a passion for precision and scalp health, Shalom specializes in protective styling — from tight, uniform cornrows to soft, boho-textured knotless braids. Every client leaves with a scalp-friendly install and a style built to last.

Braids by Shalom is a home studio in Ngong, run on appointment, one client at a time — because a good braid needs full attention, not a rushed chair.
